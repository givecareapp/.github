## 👋 About

**53 million Americans are family caregivers.** They provide $600B in unpaid care annually, but they're 2-3x more likely to develop depression, 50% more likely to have chronic conditions, and lose an average of $300K in lifetime earnings.

We're building open-source tools to help them measure burnout, track progress, and find support.

## 🎯 What We're Building

**Open-source projects for caregiver support:**

- **📊 GC-SDOH-28 Assessment** — Validated 28-question caregiver burden measurement
- **🤖 AI Conversation Patterns** — Empathetic support chatbot frameworks
- **📈 Burnout Tracking SDK** — Longitudinal wellbeing analytics
- **🗄️ Resource Matching** — Local service discovery algorithms
- **🚨 Crisis Detection** — Safety protocols for mental health support

Repositories will be released as they reach stable versions (Q2-Q4 2025).

## 🔬 Research Foundation

### GC-SDOH-28 Framework

Our assessment tool measures seven domains of caregiver burden:
- 💰 Financial strain
- 🤝 Social isolation
- 🏥 Healthcare access
- 🏠 Housing quality
- 👥 Community support
- ⚖️ Work-life balance
- 😰 Emotional wellbeing

**Built on:**
- REACH-II (NIH-validated, University of Pittsburgh)
- CWBS (Caregiver Well-Being Scale, © 1993 Susan Tebb)
- WHO/CDC SDOH frameworks

**Validation status:**
- ✅ Pilot testing complete (n=127)
- ✅ Internal consistency (Cronbach's α = 0.89)
- ✅ Test-retest reliability (ICC = 0.84)
- 🔄 Multi-site validation study in progress
